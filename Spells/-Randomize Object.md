This Spell transforms an object no larger than a breadbox that you can see within range into a random new form.  
An attended object held by an unwilling creature may roll a Charisma saving throw to resist.

The transformation should be as random as possible, and the caster has no control over the result of the transformation.

At the end of the spell's duration, an attended object's wielder may repeat their Charisma saving throw. On a failure, the transformation persists indefinitely.

**_M_**: a bit of clay

No Concentration
