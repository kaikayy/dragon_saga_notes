You imbue a creature you touch with positive energy to undo a debilitating effect. You can reduce the target's [[Exhaustion 1]] level by one, or end one of the following effects on the target:
- One effect that [[Charmed]] or [[Petrified]] the target
- One curse, including the target's attunement to a cursed magic item
- Any reduction to one of the target's ability scores
- One effect reducing the target's hit point maximum

**_M_**: diamond dust worth at least 100gp, which the spell consumes, 100gp

No Concentration
