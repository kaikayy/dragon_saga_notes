A veil of shadows and silence radiates from you, masking you and your companions from detection. For the duration, each creature you choose within 30 feet of you (including you) has a **+10** bonus to Stealth ==checks== and can't be tracked except by magical means. A creature that receives this bonus leaves behind no tracks or other traces of its passage.

**_M_**: ashes from a burned leaf of mistletoe and a sprig of spruce
