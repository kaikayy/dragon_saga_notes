Name:
Feast of the Moon

Date:
Between [[Uktar]] 30 & [[Nightal]] 1

Purpose:
Start of Winter.

Activity:
Celebrate and honor ancestors and respected dead.
Priests of a number of deities of various pantheons held rites, ceremonies, and festivals.
[[Heralds]]:
Perform the "Bloodsong", reciting genealogies of noble families.

Religious Activity:
[[Bhaal]]:
Recount tales of heroic slayings.
[[Kelemvor]]:
Tell "Deeds of the Dead" to preserve their memory.
[[Lliira]]:
see: [[Midsummer]]
[[Myrkul]]:
Believe the essence of the dead roam the land as ghosts.
[[Savras]]:
24 hour meditation in steam baths.
[[Shar]]:
Plot nefarious deeds for next year.
[[Talos]]:
Call for storms and lightning.
[[Tempus]]:
Honor the dead.

Etc.:
Most battles ended for winter leaving many dead soldiers for this celebration.
