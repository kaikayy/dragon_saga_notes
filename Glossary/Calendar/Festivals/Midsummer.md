Name:
Midsummer

Date:
Between [[Flamerule]] 30 & [[Eleasis]] 1

Purpose:
Celebration of Summer.

Activity:
Midsummer Night:
Feasting, music-making, frolicking.
[[Silverymoon]]:
Feast of Love.
[[Harpers]]:
Meet with relatives and church allies.

Religious Activity:
[[Beshaba]]:
Engage in revels of rudeness and destruction.
[[Lathander]]:
Song of Dawn.
[[Lliira]]:
Holy Festival, throwing swords an ground with flowers.
[[Loviatar]]:
"Rite of Pain and Purity", dancing on broken glass.
[[Mielikki]]:
"Wild Ride" , celebrate sensual aspect of life and planting rites.
[[Milil]]:
"Grand Revel", feasting, dancing, parodies and satire.
[[Oghma]]:
Make or renew contracts.
[[Silvanus]]:
Give sacrifice by breaking and burying objects made of wood.
[[Sune]]:
Play flirtatious games and pranks.
[[Talos]]:
Call for storms and lightning.
[[Tymora]]:
Night of daring adventure, wild revels, pranks and private romance.

Etc.:
It is believed that deities ensured good weather.
Bad weather = very bad omen.
Bad weather is very rare.
