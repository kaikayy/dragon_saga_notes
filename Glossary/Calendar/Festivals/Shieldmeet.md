Name:
Shieldmeet / Cinnaeloscor

Date:
Day after [[Midsummer]], once every 4 years

Purpose:
Leap year day.

Activity:
Varied greatly across the lands.

Religious Activity:
[[Beshaba]]:
Continue reveling.
[[Deneir]]:
"The Gliding", priests emboss letters "Deneir" in gold.
[[Helm]]:
"Ceremony of Honor"
[[Kelemvor]]:
Tell the "Deeds of the Dead" to preserve their memory.
[[Mielikki]]:
"Wild Ride" continues.
[[Oghma]]:
see: [[Midsummer]]
[[Selûne]]:
"Conjuring of the Second Moon", summons Shards.
[[Torm]]:
Celebrate as time of agreements and oaths.
[[Tymora]]:
Sponsor grand festival in [[Yartar]].

Etc.:
The elves call this day Cinnaeloscor ("the Day of [[Corellon]]'s Peace")
