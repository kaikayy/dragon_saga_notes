Name:
Greengrass / The Greening

Date:
Between [[Tarsakh]] 30 & [[Mirtul]] 1

Purpose:
First day of Spring.

Activity:
Wealthy bring flowers to less wealthy, who spread or wear them to pray for summer.

Religious Activity:
[[Chauntea]]:
Hedonistic fertility festival.
[[Eldath]]:
Gather and have a festival.
[[Lliira]]:
Holy Festival, throwing swords an ground with flowers.
[[Loviatar]]:
"Rite of Pain and Purity", dancing on broken glass.
[[Mielikki]]:
"Wild Ride" and planting rites.
[[Milil]]:
Sing the Call to the Flowers.
[[Silvanus]]:
Breaking and burying objects made of wood.
[[Sune]]:
Frolic outdoors.
[[Talos]]:
Call for storms and lightning.
