Name:
Winter Solstice

Date:
[[Nightal]] 20

Purpose:
Shortest day of the Year.

Activity:
Simril in Waterdeep:
After dark, locate starts associated with ones birth. Stay awake all night, gather at bonfires, play music, warm drinks and food.

Religious Activity:
[[Auril]]:
Celebrate "Coming Storm" by calling vicious ice storms to torment a town. 
[[Mielikki]]:
Fourth Feast and praise in song.
[[Malar]]:
"High Hunt", armor themselves with trophies and stalk human male until his death or dawn. Whichever comes first.

Etc.:
Cloudy weather was considered especially favorable on Simril, as finding one's star on an overcast sky was viewed as a blessing from [[Tymora]].
