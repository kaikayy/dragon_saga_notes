#Location/City #Location/Sword_Coast 

City on the [[Sword Coast]].
bustling, cultured, and cosmopolitan city-state
Known for its crafts folk and gardeners, the city's multi-colored-glass lamps, precision water clocks, exquisite jewelry, and magnificent gardens ensured the warm winters were colorful and the summers were rich with fresh fruit.

Fun Fact:
Many believe it is called Neverwinter because its harbor remains ice-free in the winters, but it is actually derives from the cities gardens which stay lush and blooming even throughout winter snow.
