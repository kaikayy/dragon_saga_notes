Library and School in the [[Sword Coast]]

While in Candlekeep to be accepted as a scholar each new members has to bring something new to candlekeep which can be a book, magic item, artifact etc. As a reward you will be accepted as a member but the person who brings back the most valuable finding will be able to choose his mentor in candlekeep.
