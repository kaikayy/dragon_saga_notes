_Wondrous Item, uncommon_

This small packet contains **8** marble-sized pellets, which float or rest near where the dust was sprinkled. The pellet's weight is negligible.

Someone can use an action to smash the pellet against a hard surface to release a 15-foot cube of water. Doing so ends that pellet's magic.
