_Armor (Any Medium or Heavy Except Hide), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)_
![[Ruidium_Items.png|300]]
This magic armor has a dull, rusty color or has veins of ruidium running through it. While you wear this armor, you gain the following benefits:
- You have resistance to psychic damage.
- You can breathe water.
- You gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed.

_**Ruidium Corruption.**_ When you roll a 1 on a saving throw while wearing this armor, you must make a DC 15 Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, you gain [[Exhaustion 1]]. If you are not already suffering from ruidium corruption, you become corrupted when you fail this save.

_**If Ruidium Is Destroyed.**_ If the Apotheon is killed or redeemed, all the ruidium in Exandria is destroyed instantly, and _ruidium armor_ becomes [[Armor, +1]].
