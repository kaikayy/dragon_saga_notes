#Item 

**Residuum** is the residue of arcane energy, manifesting as a green dust obtained from [[Whitestone]] that is useful in several magical applications.

Type: Refined Ore
Components: Whitestone
Color: Green
Uses: Magic Item Creation, Spell Component Substitution, [[Suude]] Production
Cost: 500gp per pound
