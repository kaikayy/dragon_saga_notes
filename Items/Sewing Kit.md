A sewing kit is a handy and compact package that allows you to cleave materials together with needle and thread. This can include and is not limited to cloth or heavier materials like human flesh, which may use curved needles and sturdier thread, or embellishments like buttons and ornaments. Proficiency with a set of artisan's tools lets you add your proficiency bonus to any ability checks you make using the tools in your craft.

Cost: 2 sp
