#Item/Drug

This tarry substance harvested from the dark boughs of the death's head willow is a powerful intoxicant.
It can be smoked as a concentrate or injected directly into the bloodstream.
A creature subjected to a dose of black sap cannot be [[Charmed]] or [[Frightened]] for **1d6** hours.
For each dose of black sap consumed, a creature must succeed on a ==DC== 15 Constitution saving throw or become [[Poisoned]] for **2d4** hours—an effect that is cumulative with multiple doses.
Black sap is valued at 300gp per dose.
