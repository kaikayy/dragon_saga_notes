#Item/Drug
Dangerous Narcotic

Suude is an illegal substance sold across Exandria.
It is refined from arcane residuum, and causes a head rush and enhances senses and magic.

Sold by [[Bas and Kovra]] in [[Ank'Harel]]

Type: Drug
Components: Refined [[Residuum]]
Color: Brown, Blue, or Red
Uses: Magical stimulation
Cost: 80gp per dose

After burning suude within a vessel and inhaling its fumes as an action, you take 22 (**4d10**) psychic damage and must make a ==DC== 10 Constitution saving throw.
On a failure, you become [[Poisoned]] for 1 hour and fall [[Unconscious]] for 1 minute, or until you are shaken awake as an action.
During this time, the world seems to move in slow motion, and your vision is twisted in kaleidoscopic colors—yet your focus is never distorted. 
f one dose of suude, or more, is used within 1 hour of another, the ==DC== of the Constitution saving throw is increased by 5 per additional dose.
If you fail this save by 10 or more, you instantly drop to 0 hit points.

On a success, you gain the ability to use one of the following Metamagic options as a sorcerer for the next minute, requiring no sorcery points. The type of Metamagic you gain access to depends on the type of suude, and once you use it, you can’t use it again until you inhale suude again.

Brown suude allows you to use the [Extended Spell](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/basic-rules/classes#ExtendedSpell) Metamagic option.
Blue suude allows you to use [Twinned Spell](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/basic-rules/classes#TwinnedSpell).
Red suude grants [Distant Spell](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/basic-rules/classes#DistantSpell).
