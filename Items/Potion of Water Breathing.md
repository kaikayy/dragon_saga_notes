#Item/Potion #Item/Uncommon 
_Potion, uncommon_
![[Potion_of_Water_Breathing_PNG.png|300]]
You can breathe underwater for 1 hour after drinking this potion. Its cloudy green fluid smells of the sea and has a jellyfish-like bubble floating in it.
Administering a potion takes an action.
Drinking a potion takes a bonus action.
Using a potion out of Combat allows the maximum effect of the potion.
