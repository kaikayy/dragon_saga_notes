#Item/Potion #Item/Common 
_Potion, common_
Cost: 50 gp
Weight: 1/2 lb

A character who drinks the magical red fluid in this vial regains **2d4+2** (max 10) hit points. Administering a potion takes an action.
Drinking a potion takes a bonus action.
Using a potion out of Combat allows the maximum effect of the potion.
