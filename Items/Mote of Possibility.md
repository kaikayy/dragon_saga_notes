#Item/Charm 
The Mote manifests as a flash of silver light as the caster touches each character’s forehead in turn.
A character who fails a saving throw or misses on an attack roll can use a reaction to expend their Mote of Possibility, allowing them to reroll the **d20**.
The character must use the new roll. A Mote of Possibility vanishes from a character after 24 hours or when it is expended.
