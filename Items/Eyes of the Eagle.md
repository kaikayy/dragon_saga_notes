_Wondrous Item, uncommon (requires attunement)_
![[Eyes_of_the_Eagle_PNG.png|300]]
These crystal lenses fit over the eyes. While wearing them, you have advantage on Perception ==checks== that rely on sight. In conditions of clear visibility, you can make out details of even extremely distant creatures and objects as small as 2 feet across.
