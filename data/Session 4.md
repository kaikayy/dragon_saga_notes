We continue our way through [[Gnomengarde]]

	- [[Venatrix]] crosses first
	- [[AhaTi]] follows
	- [[Cin]], [[Ofeus]] then [[Justen]]
- crossing requires a bit of coordination but isn't difficult
- [[Ofeus]] tells [[Cin]] feels like a real adventurer
- vision is limited due to intense fog from the cool water

We enter the next cave entrance
- a rhythmic metallic sound can be heard, like the sharpening of a blade
- two spinning blades are filling the room in front of us, spinning in opposite directions
	- [[Ofeus]] tries to look for a pattern to get through
		- [[Perception]]: 8
	- [[Venatrix]] looks around the room as best she can
		- [[Perception]]: 6
			- maybe with some acrobatic jumps and the right timing, you can get through
	- [[Venatrix]] wants to jump through, [[Cin]] stops her
	- [[Cin]] looks through his bag
	- [[Ofeus]], [[Perception]]: 7
		- nothing on the walls
	- [[Cin]], [[Perception]]: 4
		- can't make out the mechanism any closer
		- casts [[Mage Hand]] hand to put a handful of sand onto and hopefully into the mechanisms base to destroy it
			- it works
			- one of the spinning blades is stopped
- we can now enter one half of the room, with one spinning blade tower left
- [[Venatrix]] tries to jump through the second blade
	- [[DEX SAV]]: 20 - SAVE
	- makes it through and pulls a lever she sees
	- the other blades stop

The sound of the waterfall fades and we hear two voices arguing up ahead
- [[AhaTi]] leads the party toward the only way before us
- we come upon two rock gnomes arguing about contraptions to help [[King Korboz]], wielding tools
- [[AhaTi]] explains our situation and mission
	- the gnomes explain that they have their own problems with their two kings arguing and hallucinating about shapeshifters
	- they offer to give us their inventions if we can calm their king down
	- their names are [[Daddledob]], [[Fibblestib]]

Further ahead we see a podium out of reach with a book
- we ask [[Daddledob]] and [[Fibblestib]]
	- they say it's their king's spellbook
- [[AhaTi]] asks them if we can have a look inside
	- they agree as long as their king is okay with it
- we come to an three way intersection and continue forwards toward the Kings' chamber

We arrive in a bigger room with two thrones cobbled together by scrap metal sizes for gnomes
- it seems to be a dead end
- [[Venatrix]] calls out to [[King Korboz]]
	- nothing
- [[Cin]] suggests to go back and ask the gnomes from before to lead us to the right room
- The gnome corrects himself to go to the right at the intersection
- We follow the path to the right and arrive at another singular closed door
- [[AhaTi]] calls out toward the door for [[King Korboz]]
	- a voice answers
	- [[King Korboz]] talks about killed gnomes and a shapeshifter that killed them
	- [[AhaTi]] tries to convince him that the threat of the dragon is more important
	- [[Ofeus]] asks who disappeared and where
	- near the wine cellar and no bodies were found

We decide to go to the wine cellar and see for ourselves
- on the way we ask [[Daddledob]] and [[Fibblestib]] if they know of the missing gnomes
	- they do and say its not normal for them to be away that long but they are sure they'll show up
- [[Ofeus]] looks around in the room for anything out of the ordinary
	- [[Perception]]: 19
		- The southern barrels all have stopcocks on them
		- the eastern barrels seem empty
		- all others are full and untapped
	- takes a closer look at the empty barrels
		- nothing of interest
- [[Venatrix]] readies an arrow and monitors the barrels closely east to north etc
	- the wood of one barrel starts twitching and forms a giant mouth with sharp teeth
	- ### [[Combat_S4]]

[[Venatrix]] spits out some sludge and tries to rid herself of the rest of it
- [[AhaTi]] and [[Justen]] help her out
- [[Ofeus]] casts [[Prestidigitation]] to help clean up [[Venatrix]]
- [[Cin]] looks at the remnants of the [[Mimic]]
	- it seems to be turning all into purple flesh
	- [[Cin]] tries pulling out a tooth and fails
		- [[Strength STR]]: 8
- [[Venatrix]] helps cutting out a tooth after being sufficiently clean and gives it to [[Cin]]
	- she herself cuts out a piece of purple flesh, its sticky and slimy
- We talk a bit about [[AhaTi]]'s love for books and her past jobs
	- [[Cin]] offers to make her known with some library officials in a big city when we come across one
- [[AhaTi]] gives [[Venatrix]] a book "Die Magie der Schokolade" suggesting she can switch it out with the gnomes spellbook if she wants to

We go back to the locked door of the Kings' chambers
- [[Venatrix]] says that we killed the shapeshifter
- [[King Korboz]] unbinds his co-king and unlocks the door after being presented evidence
	- he presents us with two items as a reward
	- one Wizard's Hat and a Staff that throws light and sparks
	- [[Cin]] takes the items to further identify
- [[Fibblestib]] takes us to the "treasure room"
	- it consists mostly of scrap metal parts and a few inventions he presents to us
	- an extending staff, that changes from 1ft to 10ft in length with a command word
		- "Haug" for short
		- "Sûr" for long
			- [[Pole of Collapsing]]
	- a clockwork amulet that helps with "balance" while throwing or shooting
		- [[Clockwork Amulet]]
	- he leads us to the tome we saw before that should be their spellbook
		- we find a few spells we can recognize in it

		- [[detect magic]]
		- [[identify]]
		- [[mage armor]]
		- [[magic missile]]

We gather in the sleeping quarters
